FraudSpeechAlignment

A small English-language sample set of simulated telecom fraud-call recordings, used to align speech and text for fraud-call detection research. Each clip below is a synthetic re-enactment of a common scam scenario, labelled by category.

Note: These recordings are synthetic / staged samples created for research and model-training purposes — they do not contain real victims' data. Content depicts scam scripts (bank, lottery, kidnapping, phishing, etc.) purely to help build and evaluate fraud-detection systems.

Part of the FraudSpeechAlignment repository. Audio sourced from the TeleAntiFraud sample set (English, fraud-only subset).
